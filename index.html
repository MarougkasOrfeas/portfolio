About me

I'm a Full-Stack Web Developer at EUROPEAN DYNAMICS, specialising in Java and Angular. For more than four years I've been building and maintaining web applications across multiple projects, including contributions to the European Union's EU Law Tracker platform.

I have a solid foundation in software engineering principles, object-oriented programming and end-to-end web development, from Spring Boot microservices and RabbitMQ messaging to Angular front ends, containerised with Docker and Kubernetes.

I enjoy new challenges and I'm always learning new technologies. I studied Informatics and Computer Engineering at the University of West Attica.
